How it works

Set it up once, then live your day.

  1. Answer a few questions

    Wake time, bedtime, when your energy crashes and how you take your coffee. ARC shows your chronotype and your first day before you choose a plan. No account.

  2. Do one thing at a time

    Each part of the day gets one sentence about what your body is doing and one thing worth doing, in the app and on your home screen.

  3. Tap when you crash

    One tap. After about ten days ARC plans around the dip it measured, and Trends shows whether your crashes are falling.

Questions

Before you download.

Do I need a wearable?

No. ARC works from what you tell it in setup and a few taps a day. There’s nothing to wear or connect.

Does ARC need my location?

No. It estimates sunrise from your time zone, so it never asks for location access.

Where is my data kept?

Your log is stored on your iPhone. There’s no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

How is the coffee cutoff worked out?

From your bedtime, how fast you clear caffeine and every drink you log, so that less than {{TARGET_MG}} mg is still in you when you go to bed. It moves each time you log a cup.

Why no sleep score or streaks?

On purpose. ARC tells you what your body is doing and what helps. It doesn’t grade your day.

Does it work for shift workers?

Yes, if your schedule is steady: set your own wake time and bedtime and the whole day follows. Rotas that change every few days are much harder to plan around.

Which languages?

English and German.

How do I cancel?

In iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then ARC. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends and you won’t be charged again.

Is ARC medical advice?

No. ARC is a wellness app, not a treatment. If tiredness is affecting your health, talk to a doctor.
